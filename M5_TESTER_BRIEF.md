# RPG³ Playtest Brief: Angel's Rest & North-Ridge

Welcome to **Angel's Rest**. You are unlicensed rookies reporting to the East-Gate Rookery on the frontier of Animus, where four peoples share hearth and guild board alongside wild Spirit-beasts called **Sprites**. Your goal is to clear two local Guild contracts, bond a **Prime Starter** companion, and earn your **East-Gate Clearance License**.

---

## 1. Launch & Table Setup (Zero Download)

- **Play in Browser:** https://anima7ed.github.io/rpg-cubed-playtest/
- **Recommended Group Mode:** One host or Game Master opens the table and shares their window on Discord or Zoom while players call their moves.
- **Multi-Browser Room Mode:** Click **Host Room URL** in the top bar and share the `#room=` link so each player claims their own seat live. Use **Save JSON** at breaks to keep a backup snapshot.
- **10-Minute Solo Warm-Up:** Switch the top preset menu to **Walkthrough #1** first to play a guided three-round skirmish.

## 2. What You Hold: The 5-Layer Cube

Every character is built on five `5x5` grid layers rather than a paper stat sheet:

1. **Attribute Layer (Stats & Life in One):** `Health`, `Strength`, `Finesse`, and `Arcane` grow from the four corners; `Spirit` touches any attribute. Touching same-type tiles grant bonus points (**1 tile = 1, 2 touching = 3, 3 touching = 5**). Damage **flips tiles face-down**, lowering that stat live; losing your last live tile defeats you.
2. **Experience & Reputation Layer:** Holds **Experience** tiles (for Skills and Talents) and **Reputation** tiles (for Guild gear).
3. **Skill Layer:** Learned Skills sit here. Flip an un-flipped Skill tile face-down once per site for a `0`-die emergency burst.
4. **Equipment Layer (`2` Active Hands Max):** Face-up gear grants Armor/Shield Defense and weapon stats. Keep at most **2 Hands** face-up at once; flip backup gear face-down to stow it.
5. **Inventory Layer:** Holds field supplies, raw harvest stock, Arcane Crystals, and your **Keepsake Blank**.

**Only Actions are cards.** Your hand is dealt automatically from the face-up Skills, Gear, and Bonded Partner on your Cube.

## 3. One Combat Round in Four Steps

1. **Prepare:** Check your five `d10` Attribute dice (`Ready`, `Spent`, or `Dark` when all tiles of that stat are flipped), your **Target Defense** (`Live Health + Armor/Shield + Active Partner`), and your `2` Active Hands.
2. **Declare (Sprites First):** Wild Sprites lock their actions **first** on the Turn Board so you can read the threat. Each player then clicks **Declare** on one Action Card (`1` weapon attack max per turn).
3. **React:** Spend `1` unspent Move die (`Strength`, `Finesse`, or `Arcane`), or flip a Skill tile for its `0`-die burst, to pivot hex facing (`60` or `180` degrees) and cancel a Rear ambush.
4. **Resolve & Soak (Bottom Bar):**
   - **1-Die Attack Walkover:** If your Attack Attribute strictly beats **Target Defense** (and the target is not in Active Guard or Poor Visibility), spend `1` Ready die for an automatic hit.
   - **2d10 Slider Roll:** Otherwise, pick a **Difficulty Slider (`1` to `10`)** and roll **Cost** (`<= Slider`) and **Effect** (`>= Slider`). Both must pass. Low sliders (`1` to `5`) risk Cost failure to earn **Effect Points** (`+1` flip each on hits); high sliders (`6` to `10`) earn **Cost Thrift** (`2` points keep `1` rolled die Ready). Spend matching edge points or **Consume** (flip) `1` live tile after rolling to rescue a close miss.
   - **Soak & Site Break:** Flip `1` worn Armor or Shield piece (**Soak**) to absorb `1` hit flip. Out of combat, the party gets **1 Site Break per site**: flipped Skills restore automatically, and each character rolls one `0`-die recovery check (`Health` Cost + `1` Attribute Effect) to un-flip Attribute tiles.

## 4. Recommended 90-Minute Run & Telemetry Export

1. **Clear 2 Red Contracts** (`Quests` tab: `2` pooled Experience + `1` Reputation per contract by open party vote; your first `Spirit` tile unlocks your **Fresh-Orange** band).
2. **Bond a Prime Starter** (`0` Crystals required) and pass your **Clearance Exam** against **Sergeant Hal** (`Track A`) or **Collina** on North-Ridge (`Track B`, where a `1`-die Finishing Blow sparks `4` Boss Surge rounds of rapid harvesting).
3. **Export Telemetry (2 Minutes):** Open the **Log** tab, enter your **Mode**, **Cool (`1-10`)**, and **Fun (`1-10`)** scores plus `2` or `3` table notes, and click **Export Telemetry (.json)**.
