SPATIAL TENSION

Adjacency Beats Arithmetic

Health, Strength, Finesse, and Arcane root in the four corners while Spirit bridges neighbors. A lone tile scores 1, two touching score 3, and three touching score 5. Every hit you take is a tactical choice over which cluster you can afford to fracture.

WALKOVER OR SLIDER

Earned Mastery & Push-Your-Luck

Outclass a foe's Target Defense and you spend 1 Ready die for an automatic Walkover hit. In contested clashes, set your own Difficulty Slider (1 to 10) and roll Cost (<=) against Effect (>=) to balance extra tile flips against dice thrift.

LIVING FRONTIER

Sprites, Field Forges & Four Peoples

Wild Sprites run on the same five-layer Cube law as players. Leave a defeated Sprite helpless to open a bonding rite, or strike a 1-die Finishing Blow to harvest crystal charge, ignite a field-forge glow, and trigger four rounds of rapid North-Ridge harvesting.

How a Playtest Session Runs

  1. Open the Browser Table: Launch Web-Box-0 on desktop or tablet. For zero-friction group play, one host shares their window on Discord or Zoom; for multi-browser tables, click Host Room URL to share a live seat link.
  2. Run Two Contracts & Bond a Partner: Pick two local Guild contracts from the East-Gate Quest Board, vote openly on pooled Experience, place your first Spirit tile, and bond one of six Prime Starters.
  3. Pass Your Clearance Exam: Spar Sergeant Hal in a non-lethal coaching bout or test your Cube against Collina on the North-Ridge hex map.
  4. Export Your Telemetry JSON: Open the Log tab, score Cool and Fun (1 to 10), jot two honest friction or highlight notes, and click Export Telemetry (.json).
